# The scenes the game can load, and what each one is made of.
#
# Five fields, all required. `-` means none. Blank lines and `#` comments are
# fine. See src/scene/scene_list.h for field details.
#
# spawn is `terrain` (stand on ground under spawn column) or `floor`
# (stand on world border). A scene without a material map must use `floor`.
#
# Everything after the five is a named field, `key=value`, in any order, each
# optional and each at most once:
#   mode=<mode>    `fixed` (the default) or `unclamped` (the camera may scroll
#                  past the world's side edges).
#   size=<W>x<H>   the world in cells. Without one, the material map's size.
#   scale=<N>      screen pixels per cell, 1 to 64. Without one, 4.
#   level=<file>   what the level puts in the world: player column, objective,
#                  enemies (src/scene/level_list.h). Without one the player
#                  starts mid-world, the objective goes an eighth of the world in
#                  from the far edge, and enemies are spread by a heuristic.
#   backdrop=<dir> the painted layers behind the scene: assets/<dir>/backdrop.txt
#                  (src/render/backdrop_set.h). Without one, the clear colour.
